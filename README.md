# Air Quality Data Analysis Dashboard

This repository contains a data analysis project focused on monitoring and analyzing the Air Quality Index (AQI) and various pollutant levels across major cities in India between 2015 and 2020. The dashboard provides a high-level overview of pollution trends, geographical distribution, and city-wise comparisons.

## 💡 Key Insights & Findings

Based on the visualized data, several key trends and metrics were identified:

* **Overall Pollutant Averages:** The dataset shows average concentrations of PM2.5 at 61.33, NO at 17.02, SO2 at approximately 11.51, and CO at 0.98.


* **Most Polluted Cities:** Delhi ranks as the most polluted city with an average AQI of 251.83, followed by Patna (169.36) and Amritsar (129.17).


* **Least Polluted Cities:** Chandigarh reports the lowest average AQI (93.24) among the tracked locations, with Amaravati (101.50) and Hyderabad (104.86) following closely.


* **Historical Trends:** The AQI trend overview indicates a sharp decline in average AQI from 2015 to 2016, followed by a slight rise in 2017, and a steady, gradual decrease through 2020.


* **Geographical Distribution:** The "Area Wise AQI" map highlights regional pollution clusters, showing severe concentrations in northern and eastern regions compared to the south.
